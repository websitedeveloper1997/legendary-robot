Frequently asked questions

How long is the session?

75 minutes, one-on-one, so the time goes to your questions and your situation.

What will we cover?

Business formation, licensing and enrollment, policies, caregiver recruitment, client intake and operations, and marketing. We focus on the areas that matter most to you.

Do you only help with Medicaid agencies?

No. We cover private pay and Medicaid models, so you can choose the one that fits your goals.

I have not started yet. Is that okay?

Yes. The session is built for people at the start, and also for agencies that need more structure.

What happens after I send the form?

We contact you by email or phone to agree on a time for your session.
